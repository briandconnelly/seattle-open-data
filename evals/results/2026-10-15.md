## Findings

- **Fixed in this run:** the SPD Crime guide said neighborhood counts "undercount badly". A by-year probe after task 14 showed `-` covers 98–100% of offenses from 2008 to 2016 but only 1–4% since 2017. The guide and the coverage-warning reason now say so.
- **Not exercised:** multi-page cursor paging (task 11 fit one page), the `column_not_geographic` error path (task 07 read `geo_unusable` first instead), and the rate-limit path. All three are covered by recorded-fixture tests instead.
- **Agent behavior outside the server:** in tasks 08 and 12, the host allowed Bash, and the agent went around the server once it had told the agent what it could not do. That is a host permission choice, not a server defect.
